title: Indian Cinema in Context

---

As its title suggests, _Indian Cinema in Context_ introduces students to the Indian film industry, emphasizing how the movies it produces fit in the broad cultural and political landscape of South Asia. The course spans the full history of the industry, from the arrival of film technology to British India in the silent-film era to the surge of international interest in a shiny, globalized “Bollywood.”

Along the way, students will learn the basic terminology and techniques of film studies, as well as get a general introduction to South Asian history in the 20<sup>th</sup> century. By the end of the course, students will have a grasp of the general organization of the many Indian film industries, and will understand the major phases and movements of the Hindi film industry in particular. Particular attention will be paid to how films have been used as a way to project, cultivate, and reconcile various competing images of the Indian nation.
