---
title: Introduction to Composition
school: Bradley University
summary: A first-year introduction to college writing, with a focus on genre and
  audience.
description: >-
  * Clear, concise, and well motivated explanations

  * Use diverse sources to fulfill the multiple roles required by the genre (provide background,  position the argument within a broader conversation, illustrate a point, support with factual evidence, provide a conceptual framework)

  * Provide attentive, detail-driven evaluations that stand alone

  * Analyze texts not just for their meaning but for their action in the world, their place and purpose.
