A yearlong introduction to the Sanskrit language, Elementary Sanskrit covers not only the fundamentals of grammar from the epic and classical period but also cultural and literary background that can help students contextualize and appreciate the significance of the texts they are learning to read. The course focuses on helping you build the skills needed to read historical texts, but it also includes some spoken exercises and contemporary readings to encourage fluency and to give a sense of how the language lives on in India today, and not just as a "dead" language – at least not in way we stereotypically think of dead languages.

The year will culminate in a reading directly from the _Ramayana_, one of Sanskrit's great epics. Students can expect to leave the course able to read simple texts with a dictionary and more challenging texts with the aid of a translation as well.
